## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Add browser-local Bilibili AV1/HEVC/AVC video with AAC audio and danmaku through an instance-scoped Tampermonkey helper. Each participant uses their own platform access. Previewing, cancelling, or rejecting a replacement link preserves the current playback connection and selected video. Player failures remain recoverable without disconnecting the room. Background loading tolerates browser-deferred media initialization and continues when the page returns to the foreground. When wait for everyone is enabled, buffer seeks during playback and resume automatically without a manual pause. Support exact multipart matching, a Members/Episodes sidebar for multipart videos and collections with host selection for the room and current-episode marking, room controls and waiting, local selection of playable qualities including high-bitrate premium tracks, exact browser codec checks with automatic AV1 → HEVC → AVC preference at the selected quality and a badge showing the loaded codec, chunked streaming of large fragments without a 64 MiB cutoff, correct progression across reordered frame boundaries, bounded prefetch with buffer reclamation and local expiry refresh that preserves the selected encoding, and local comment visibility, density, text size, 0.5×–1.5× scrolling speed, and full-screen, top-half (default), or top-quarter display areas for scrolling comments, remembered in the browser across reloads and rooms. Fixed top and bottom comments retain their positions when the area changes. Comments use stable lanes with space between texts, skip crowded timestamps, and keep clear of player controls. At the default 1× speed, every scrolling comment crosses in ten seconds; longer text moves faster to cover its longer path, and speed changes preserve visible positions. Keep comments visible through pause position corrections without reloading the current comment segment. Danmaku settings fit their content without horizontal scrolling. Refreshing during playback waits for the current room position to buffer without a false synchronization failure. Hover over the danmaku toggle for its settings, with keyboard Down and touch long-press access; click still toggles comments, and no separate player settings button is shown. Remember local quality and volume choices, default to the highest playable quality and unmuted 50% volume, and offer a dedicated quality menu. Detect outdated helper builds even when development release versions match, and show helper installation reminders as bottom toasts and activate new installations through Reload page. Use a permitted HTTPS CDN backup when a track's primary address is unsupported, and distinguish unsupported media addresses from network or helper-permission failures. Helper failures show grouped next steps. A participant without platform access stays unready and appears to others as Platform access unavailable. Support helper installation and detection on both localhost and 127.0.0.1 during frontend hot reload.
- Add local synchronized viewing with browser-only file selection, sampled media matching over HTTP, authenticated room controls, automatic playback takeover between pages, clock calibration, drift correction, and reconnect. Keep playback and synchronization active when the page is backgrounded, and refresh calibration on return without interrupting playback. On a high-latency link, the page states that synchronization may drift, and waits and room controls stay available. Support startup and buffering waits that name the participants being waited for, ready late-join catch-up, and controller permission during host absence, with the reason when room controls are unavailable. Keep the player layout and button appearance stable while commands await acknowledgement; offer an additional recovery overlay only after more than three seconds without a command result, independently of media loading or readiness. Cancelling a play attempt keeps media usable without a playback error. When Enable playback disappears, keyboard focus stays in the player. Playback restarts paused at zero; persistent-write failures leave existing playback available.
- Add persistent anonymous identities and private rooms with reusable invitation links/codes, host/everyone controls and wait-for-everyone preferences, member removal, leave, closure, immediate session revocation, and bounded expired-session cleanup. Support direct HTTP with session/CSRF protection and isolated browser profiles. While the room remains open, invitations survive failed joins, helper installation, reloads, departure, member removal, and elapsed time without expiry. Pending invitation entry resumes after reload. Only host replacement or room closure invalidates an invitation; hosts can return through the room URL or current invitation. Empty rooms still close after the normal departure grace. Confirm departure before leaving. Keyboard operation keeps focus on the current task, status text is readable, server status distinguishes unreachable from unable to save, and room endings, connection, storage, and session failures each give their own recovery step.
- Add an embedded Vue interface with English and Simplified Chinese support, following the device language by default with English fallback and immediate browser-local language overrides. Home shows one of five neutral greetings or short feature introductions per visit, with a compact heading and clear room-creation guidance. Keep the selected heading stable while entering data or switching languages. Use a muted Slate blue default and eight additional accents. Default to dark mode with manual light/dark choices that survive reloads. Group appearance, language, notifications, and About in a settings menu, leaving server status, settings, and identity on the right of the header. About shows the deployed sync, web, and helper component versions, the build’s Git commit with a dirty label for uncommitted source changes, and an exact current-commit tag when available. Cmd/Ctrl + K opens searchable quick actions for video selection, permitted playback and room controls, and local preferences. Support English/Chinese keywords, `/` commands, and `$` or direct Bilibili link/BV input that prefills the existing confirmation flow. Click the video to play/pause; use Space/K, Left/Right, Up/Down, M, D, and F across the foreground page without focusing the player, including after pointer seeking. Keep native input, menu, and dialog keyboard behavior. Outside these surfaces, Space controls playback even after clicking a button or closing a dialog, without moving focus; Enter still activates buttons. Use subtle color feedback without focus outlines across the interface, and preserve focused controls while playback commands complete. Use Up/Down for 10% volume steps and D for danmaku, with distinct visibility icons. Keep volume out of player settings. Support Enter to confirm video changes, show dismissible bottom toasts with a saved left/center/right position, open the OVS logo in a new tab, simplify profile and footer controls, distinguish the room settings icon, and bound desktop room width by available player height and sidebar visibility to preserve 16:9 playback on ultrawide or short windows. Dialog transitions and subtle button feedback respect reduced motion and keep keyboard actions immediate. Include HTTP health checks, validated configuration, and SQLite migrations with exclusive data-directory ownership.
- Add development, validation, and release commands, plus a non-root application container with persistent `/data` storage. Remove repository-local `data/` with `make clean`, `make uninstall`, or `make build CLEAN=1`; preserve custom data directories. Combine the sync, web, and helper versions in image tags, and use the same tag for local builds and SSH deployment with support for an explicit `IMAGE` override. Deploy and manage remote Linux servers over SSH with Docker, automatic Compose preparation, and optional Caddy HTTPS for public IPs or domains. Show numbered deployment and management steps, remote substeps, status, and elapsed time. Show transfer progress with percentage bars for known sizes and an activity bar with byte counts and average throughput for streamed images; keep redirected logs plain. Preserve data across updates and verify public HTTPS without accepting untrusted certificates. Keep manual and existing-proxy deployment support. Optional trusted-proxy CIDRs keep per-client request limits behind a reverse proxy.
